# Code of Conduct

Replay welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and ways of contributing. Everyone should be able to ask questions, report problems, and discuss changes without harassment or personal attacks.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient. Not everyone has the same devices, language skills, or technical experience.
- Discuss the code, design, or behavior being reviewed—not the worth or motives of the person involved.
- Give actionable feedback and accept corrections in good faith.
- Respect other people's time. Search existing discussions, provide useful context, and avoid repeated demands for updates.
- Credit other people's work and respect their privacy.
- Follow reasonable moderation requests and help keep discussions focused.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or discriminatory remarks.
- Sexual harassment or unwelcome sexual content.
- Personal insults, targeted humiliation, or sustained disruption of a discussion.
- Publishing private information, messages, credentials, or identifying details without consent.
- Spam, impersonation, retaliation against someone making a report, or attempts to evade moderation.

Disagreement and criticism are welcome when expressed constructively. A rejected feature request or technical disagreement is not a reason to target another participant.

## Scope

This policy applies to this repository's issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-managed spaces. It also applies when someone is explicitly representing Replay in a public space.

## Reporting a concern

Contact the [project maintainer](https://github.com/Riteshp2001) through a private contact method they make available. Include relevant links, a brief account of what happened, and any immediate concerns.

If no private contact method is listed, open a brief issue asking for a private reporting channel. Do not include names, sensitive evidence, or the details of the report in that public request. You can also use GitHub's built-in reporting tools for conduct on GitHub.

Share only the information needed to review the concern. Do not repost a report publicly or encourage others to target the people involved.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, ask participants to change their behavior, close or lock discussions, or restrict participation when needed. The response should reflect the context, severity, and any repeated behavior.

Reports should be handled with care for the privacy and safety of everyone involved. Someone with a conflict of interest should step back from reviewing a report when another maintainer is available. Requests to reconsider a moderation decision should use the same private reporting route and provide relevant context.
